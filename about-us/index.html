Our Founder’s Story

The courage to begin.
The conviction to begin again.

01

An unexpected door

William Lee’s future seemed settled. With his chemical engineering studies supported by an IOI Group scholarship, he had entered a respected profession and worked at a palm oil mill. To his family and friends, engineering offered security and a future worth holding on to.

But a personal purchase opened an unexpected door.

When William wanted to tint his car, he researched it with an engineer’s attention to detail. He discovered complex technology, yet sales conversations often focused on price, a single infrared rejection figure and warranty years. Customers had little guidance to understand what they were buying.

02

The courage to begin

His curiosity became a passion—and eventually, a decision to leave engineering rather than return to IOI Group. His family questioned why he would walk away from such a secure career.

William saw the opportunity for a revolution: to make knowledge, honesty and opportunity the foundation of automotive service.

With a young team of three, P10X began through honest consultation, careful workmanship and a willingness to learn from customers.

03

The conviction to begin again

Then came a defining test. The second outlet in Bangsar faced financial pressure, while the team fell short of the commitment and standards the business needed. William made the difficult decision to let the outlet team go and rebuild with the original three. Together, they kept the outlet alive.

04

Progress through people

That belief in rebuilding—and in people’s potential—continues today.

P10X’s Head of Technical began as a junior technician whose abilities were underestimated because he lacked an academic background. He progressed to leading five people, then 60 technicians. Today, he is a trainer certified by government and global brands, and an academy coach who has trained more than 200 technicians by 2026.

From three people to more than 100, William’s ambition has grown with the company: to establish standards that shape the industry and open paths for others to succeed.
