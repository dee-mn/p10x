01 / OUR GROWTH JOURNEY

From One Shop-lot to a Nationwide Ecosystem.

One branch became a connected business. Explore the people, places, and turning points that shaped P10X.

2019 — 2026

PJ Uptown

The foundation

P10X began with the takeover of the RhinePro PJ Uptown branch. Technical skill and genuine customer care became the foundation of a business built on trust.

PJ Uptown

Bangsar

Expanding our footprint

We assumed management of RhinePro Bangsar, bringing the same approach to a wider customer base and strengthening our retail operations.

RhinePro Bangsar

Bandar Sunway

The birth of the ecosystem

Our first outlet under the P10X name opened in Bandar Sunway. P10X Academy was established to develop skilled technicians and raise standards across the industry.

Bandar Sunway

Taman Equine

Specialist expertise. Wider reach.

The Quad Film branch brought specialised film solutions to Taman Equine. P10X Maison and P10X Media extended the business into distribution and content creation.

Taman Equine

3M Pro Shop

A new standard of protection

P10X was appointed to operate Malaysia’s first 3M Pro Shop in Taman Equine, pairing recognised protection products with skilled installation.

3M Pro Shop

P10X Academy

Skills recognised nationally

The network expanded with 3M Pro Shops in Kepong and Sunway. P10X Academy’s TVET milestone was marked by its official launch by YB Steven Sim on 17 June 2025.

P10X Academy

P10X Car Check

Confidence before the first drive

Car Check adds pre-delivery inspection to the ecosystem, helping owners identify aesthetic defects and understand their vehicle’s condition before handover.

P10X Car Check
