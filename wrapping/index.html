Three ways to change the feeling

Find your finish.

It is not only about colour.
It is how that colour meets the light.

01 / Gloss

Let the light play.

Clear reflections and a polished presence. Gloss gives colour a lively, paint-like look that changes as the light moves across the body.

Reflective / Expressive / Defined

Explore this direction ↗

02 / Satin

Find the quiet confidence.

Softer reflections bring out the contours with a restrained sheen. Satin sits between gloss and matte, balancing depth with a more understated presence.

Soft sheen / Subtle depth / Balance

Explore this direction ↗

03 / Matte

Let the shape speak.

A low-reflection finish puts the focus on colour and form. Matte creates a different kind of presence, where the body lines do more of the talking.

Low reflection / Shape / Character

Explore this direction ↗

From idea to installation

A bold change.
A considered approach.

01

Start with a direction.

Bring a reference, a favourite colour or just a feeling. We will explore finishes and details that suit your car and your style.

02

See it in real light.

Compare physical samples and discuss coverage. We will also assess the existing paint and talk through the preparation involved.

03

Consider every detail.

From the panel flow to the finishing touches, the installation deserves care. We will guide you through looking after the wrap, too.
