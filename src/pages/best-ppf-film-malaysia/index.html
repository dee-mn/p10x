If you’re the kind of car owner who cringes at the sight of stone chips, scratches, or swirl marks, chances are you’ve already considered Paint Protection Film (PPF). Many people search for the “best PPF brand in Malaysia” — but the truth is, finding the right PPF isn’t just about picking a name. It’s about knowing what makes a PPF film truly great: the material, performance, warranty, installation quality, and more. To help you make an informed decision, check out our 8 Best PPF Brands in Malaysia: A Guide to Best PPF Films for a detailed breakdown of top-rated options available today.

Let’s explore the top 8 car PPF brands available in Malaysia today.

Top 8 PPF International Brands in Malaysia

1. 3M (International PPF Brand from US)

3M is a pioneer in the world of PPF, originally developing urethane film for helicopter blades during the Vietnam War. Fast forward to today, 3M’s Scotchgard™ PPF is known for:

  • High clarity and gloss finish

  • Self-healing surface from minor scratches

  • TPU-based durable film

  • Comprehensive warranty and global brand trust

It’s one of the most reliable names globally and widely used by professional installers like P10X in Malaysia.

2. XPEL (International PPF Brand from US)

XPEL is another big player in the premium car protection film space. Known for its Ultimate Plus™ and Stealth™ lines, XPEL offers:

  • Self-healing TPU film

  • High gloss or satin finish

  • Pre-cut PPF with a massive vehicle pattern database

  • Long-term warranties and tested durability

XPEL is widely favoured for its brand prestige and refined aesthetics.

3. Quad Film (International PPF Brand from US)

Quad Film is a rising star with strong presence in Malaysia through P10X. It stands out by offering:

  • TPU Substrate for improved Flexibility and durability

  • Exceptional gloss and clarity

  • Pre-cut PPF support for many car models

  • Attractive pricing for full-car wrap

  • First Customise Film design for PPF

P10X trusts Quad Film for its reliability and consistent product quality — ideal for hybrid or budget-conscious PPF solutions.

4. Hexis Bodyfence (International PPF Brand from France)

Hexis is a premium film manufacturer from France, with the Bodyfence PPF line gaining popularity in Malaysia for:

  • Glossy finish with self-healing TPU

  • UV resistance and high elasticity

  • Good durability and slick feel

It’s a brand to watch if you want European-quality PPF.

5. RhinePro (International PPF Brand from US)

Although widely available in Malaysia, RhinePro is originally developed in the United States with strong R&D. It’s recognised for both innovation and premium film performance.

Key highlights:

  • Made of TPU with anti-yellowing properties

  • Use US based raw material such Ashland and Argotec

  • Gloss, matte, and coloured finishes available

  • Warranty coverage up to 10 years

RhinePro is trusted by P10X, and both brands put heavy focus on workmanship. At P10X, installers are SLDN-certified, trained through the P10X Academy to ensure high-quality application.

6. Ceramic Pro (International PPF Brand from US)

Ceramic Pro is globally renowned for its ceramic coating products, and it has taken that expertise into the PPF arena with its Kavaca Paint Protection Film line.:

  • Nano-ceramic infused PPF with instant self-healing

  • Combines benefits of PPF + ceramic coating

  • Extreme gloss and hydrophobic effect

  • Up to 12-year warranty

7. Gyeon (International PPF Brand from South Korea)

If you’re into car detailing, you’ve probably heard of Gyeon. This brand has made a big name through its quartz-based ceramic coatings and car care products is trusted globally and in Malaysia for:

  • Detailer-focused PPF with high gloss and clarity

  • Offers gloss, matte, and carbon-look options

  • Hydrophobic and self-healing features

  • ~5–10 year warranty depending on film type


8. STEK (International PPF Brand from South Korea)

STEK is known for bringing bold design into the PPF world. Their specialty PPF lines like DYNOblack and DYNOshield are:

  • Infused with nano-ceramic tech

  • High gloss and coloured variants

  • Durable with long-lasting anti-yellowing performance

Perfect for owners who want to make a statement while protecting their paint.

Key Factors to Consider When Choosing the Best PPF Films

Material Quality of PPF Film

Not all films are made equal. TPU (Thermoplastic Polyurethane) is the gold standard in car PPF — flexible, durable, and resistant to yellowing. On the other hand, TPH and PVC films are cheaper, but:

  • Tend to yellow over time

  • Lack flexibility for complex curves

  • Don’t self-heal as well

At P10X, we only use high-quality TPU films. Plus, PPF thickness (typically 7–8 Mils) and adhesive quality matter, influencing how well the film protects and lasts. Know more about PPF Type.

PPF Film Key Performance Properties

A best PPF film should offer:

  • Self-healing from fine scratches

  • Durability (up to 10 years lifespan)

  • Anti-yellowing and UV protection

  • Gloss or matte clarity

  • Hydrophobic surface for easy washing

Car PPF Installation Workmanship

Even the best film is useless without good workmanship. Key installation methods include:

  • Pre-cut PPF using digital plotters for precision

  • Hand-cut PPF by skilled technicians (with no blade contact on paint)

At P10X, our certified installers are trained via the P10X Academy under the SLDN government-recognised program. We follow industry-best practices: removing panels for seamless wrapping and ensuring no exposed edges.

PPF Film Warranty, Maintenance, and Cost

Top brands offer 5–10 year warranties, covering yellowing, cracking, or peeling. After install, avoid washing your car for a week. Long-term maintenance involves pH-neutral wash products and proper drying.

So, how much to PPF a car? Expect a full wrap to cost between RM7,000–RM20,000, depending on film quality and vehicle size. For tighter budgets, P10X offers a Hybrid PPF Package, which protects high-impact areas only.

FAQ

Does PPF brand matter?

Yes — brand affects material quality, warranty, and product consistency. But installation matters just as much.

What is the longest lasting PPF?

Premium TPU films like 3M and XPEL can last up to 10 years with proper care.

Which PPF thickness is best?

Most high-quality PPFs are 7–8 mils thick — a sweet spot for durability without bulkiness.

How to select the best PPF?

Look at the film’s self-healing, anti-yellowing, and clarity properties. Also evaluate the installer’s experience and warranty.

Which type of PPF film is the best — glossy, matte, or colour?

It depends on your style. Glossy enhances original paint, matte gives a stealthy look, and colour PPF lets you customise while protecting.
