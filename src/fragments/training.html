The learning process / Understanding into action

Watch closely.
Work carefully.
Keep improving.

A skill grows through a clear explanation, deliberate practice and honest feedback. Each step makes the next one more meaningful.

01Observe02Practise03Refine
01Understand the work

Observe the details.

Look beyond the finished surface. Learn to notice preparation, working sequence and the decisions behind a technique. Ask why a method is used before trying to repeat it.

02Put understanding to work

Practise with purpose.

Break a task into manageable steps. Focus on the way you prepare, handle tools and control a material. Repetition matters most when you know what you are trying to improve.

03Raise your own standard

Review. Refine. Repeat.

Inspect the result, recognise what needs attention and use feedback to guide the next attempt. Consistent workmanship is built by making small improvements part of the routine.

Before you begin

A clearer
starting point.

A few things to discuss with the Academy before taking your next step.

Do I need experience to enquire?

You can start the conversation at any experience level. Tell us whether you are new to automotive work or already practising a trade, and the Academy can explain the entry requirements for the relevant programme.

Which training area should I choose?

Start with the work you want to understand or improve. Share your interests, current responsibilities and goals so the team can help you assess a suitable learning pathway.

What are the duration, fees and intake dates?

These depend on the programme and current intake. Contact P10X Academy for the latest schedule, course scope, fees and what is included before making a booking.

What should I confirm about certification?

Ask the team to explain the qualification or certificate associated with your chosen programme, its assessment requirements and any eligibility criteria. Confirm these details for that specific programme before enrolling.

Can I enquire on behalf of my team?

Yes. Share your team size, current skill levels and the work you want to improve. The Academy can discuss available options and whether a suitable training arrangement can be made.
