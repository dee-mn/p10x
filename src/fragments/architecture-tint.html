01Heat control

Shade alone does not tell the whole story. Compare the film’s documented solar performance alongside the sunlight and heat your room receives.

Ask us: Which option suits this window’s exposure?

02Daylight & glare

Visible light transmission, or VLT, describes how much visible light passes through. Consider the daylight you want to keep, together with glare on screens and work surfaces.

Ask us: How will the room look with this film?

03Privacy & appearance

Tell us where you need privacy, when the space is used and whether preserving the view matters. Compare reflective, tinted and decorative options against the look you want.

Ask us: Which finish fits how this space is used?

04Film & glass suitability

The existing glazing matters. Glass type, coatings and the window’s construction should be reviewed before a film is recommended.

Ask us: What should we confirm about the existing glass?
