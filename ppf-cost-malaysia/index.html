PPF Cost and Pricing in Malaysia

PPF Cost Based On PPF Coverage Areas

Car PPF pricing in Malaysia can vary significantly depending on how much of the car you’re wrapping, and the quality of the film you choose.

Here’s a quick breakdown:

  • Full Car PPF Wrap: Expect to pay between RM10,000 to RM25,000, depending on vehicle size and film quality.

  • Ceramic Coating Package: A full car ceramic coating with window tint typically starts around RM1,700. Want a full comparison? Check out our article on [PPF vs Ceramic Coating] for the pros and cons of both.

  • Hybrid PPF Package: This is a budget-friendly combo that gives you the best of both worlds—PPF on high-impact zones (like the front bumper, hood, side mirrors) and ceramic coating on the rest of the car.

Factors that Influence the Cost of Paint Protection Film

1. PPF Material Quality and Type of Film

  • Basic PPF (often PVC or TPH): More affordable, but less durable. May yellow over time or damage the paint upon removal.

  • TPU PPF (used by P10X): Premium-grade film with self-healing, hydrophobic properties, and excellent clarity. It’s the gold standard in the market.

👉 Always ask: “What type of PPF film are you installing?”

2. PPF Coverage Area

More film = more cost. Here’s a quick guide:

  • Full Body Wrap: Covers every inch—best for full protection.

  • Partial Wrap: Focuses on high-risk areas.

  • Hybrid Package: Strategic protection + cost savings.

3. PPF Installation Complexity and Workmanship

Not all installations are equal.

  • Installation time: Full PPF can take 3–5 days depending on car complexity.

  • Curves, edges, sensors: The more intricate your car’s design, the more time and skill required.

  • Workmanship: Even the best film can fail if installed poorly.

✅ P10X Promise: Certified installers, precise workmanship, and no shortcuts.

4. Vehicle Size and Model

Bigger car = more surface area = more film and labor.

  • Compact sedans will cost less than luxury SUVs or MPVs.

  • Complex body lines or trims increase installation difficulty.


5. Type of PPF Finish

Want your car to look bold or stealthy?

  • Gloss: Most common, matches factory paint.

  • Matte: For a sleek, satin look.

  • Black PPF: Acts like a wrap with added protection.

FAQ

Is PPF worth it in Malaysia?

Yes—Malaysia’s harsh sun, rain, and dusty roads make PPF a great investment for keeping your car paint flawless.

What happens if I choose a cheaper PPF film to save money?

Lower-grade films may yellow, peel, or damage your paint during removal—leading to more costly fixes later.

Does PPF affect the original paint or car warranty?

Not when installed properly. Quality PPF like TPU is safe for OEM paint and reversible.

Why does PPF cost so much compared to ceramic coating in Malaysia?

PPF offers physical protection (against chips/scratches), whereas coating offers chemical protection. You’re paying for more material and installation time.
